About this guide

Leonida Trophy Hunter is a fan-made, offline guide. It runs entirely in your browser: your checklist is stored only on this device, nothing is uploaded, and the guide makes no network requests once loaded. Trophy names and descriptions are the official PlayStation trophy list; the how-to notes are written for this guide.
